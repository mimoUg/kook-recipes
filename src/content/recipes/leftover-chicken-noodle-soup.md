This leftover chicken noodle soup uses cooked chicken, so it is ready in about 35 minutes. Onion, carrot and celery are softened with garlic and thyme, simmered in chicken broth with egg noodles, then finished with the chicken and a squeeze of lemon. It is a warm, easy way to use up roast or rotisserie chicken.

**Recipe Details:**

- **Skill Level:** Easy
- **Prep Time:** 10 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 25 minutes
- **Total Time:** 35 minutes
- **Servings:** 6
- **Main Ingredients:** Cooked chicken, chicken broth, egg noodles, carrots, celery, onion, garlic, and thyme.
- **Recipe Source:** [UCLA Health – Leftover Chicken Noodle Soup](https://www.uclahealth.org/simms-mann-center/recipes/leftover-chicken-noodle-soup), [Alexandra's Kitchen – A Simple Recipe for Chicken Noodle Soup](https://alexandracooks.com/2019/01/25/what-to-do-with-leftover-roast-chicken-make-a-soup/)

Noodles soak up broth as they sit, so if you want leftovers of the soup itself, cook the noodles separately and add them to each bowl. Add a handful of spinach or frozen peas at the end, or use rice or small pasta instead of egg noodles.
