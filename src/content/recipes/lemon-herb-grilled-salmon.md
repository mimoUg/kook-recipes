Grilled salmon is one of those meals that feels effortlessly elegant. This version keeps things simple: a lemon-and-herb marinade, a hot grill, and a few minutes of patience. The result is flaky, juicy salmon with a bright, garlicky crust.

## Why this marinade works

Lemon juice and zest bring freshness, Dijon mustard helps the marinade cling to the fish, and a touch of honey encourages browning on the grill. Fresh parsley and dill keep it herbal rather than heavy.

## How to tell when salmon is done

Salmon is ready when the thickest part flakes easily but still looks slightly translucent in the centre. If you have a thermometer, aim for 52–54°C (125–130°F) for medium. Remember it will continue cooking as it rests.

## Serving suggestions

Serve with charred lemon, herby couscous, roasted asparagus or a simple tomato salad. Leftovers are excellent flaked into a salad or stirred through pasta the next day.
