There is something deeply satisfying about a bowl of tomato soup made from roasted tomatoes. Roasting concentrates their sweetness and gives the soup a rich, almost smoky depth that you simply cannot get from a tin.

## Why roast the tomatoes?

Roasting removes excess water and caramelises the natural sugars. The garlic becomes soft and mellow, and the onion turns sweet at the edges. It takes a little patience, but the oven does most of the work.

## Make it creamy — or don't

The soup is excellent without any cream. If you want a richer finish, stir in a splash of cream, crème fraîche or coconut milk just before serving. A swirl of olive oil and a handful of basil on top is all it really needs.

## Perfect with a cheese toastie

This is a soup that begs for a grilled cheese sandwich. Try it with sourdough, sharp cheddar and a little Dijon mustard. For a lighter meal, serve with a crisp green salad and warm focaccia.
