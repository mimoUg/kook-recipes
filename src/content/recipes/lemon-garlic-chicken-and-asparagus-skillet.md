ingredients:
  - >-
    2 cups cooked chicken cut into bite-sized pieces (rotisserie chicken,
    grilled chicken, or baked chicken all work well)
  - 1 bunch approx. 1 lb fresh asparagus, trimmed and cut into 2-inch pieces
  - 1 tablespoon olive oil
  - 2 tablespoons butter
  - ½ teaspoon garlic powder if needed
  - ½ cup chicken broth
  - ½ cup sour cream
  - 1 tablespoon lemon juice fresh is best, but bottled works
  - ½ teaspoon salt
  - ¼ teaspoon black pepper
  - 'Optional: ¼ cup grated parmesan cheese for extra flavor'

For something a little lighter and fresher, this lemon garlic chicken and asparagus skillet is a lovely option. It uses bite-sized pieces of cooked chicken and tender-crisp asparagus, brought together with a creamy sauce made from butter, chicken broth, sour cream, and lemon juice. The garlic flavor complements the bright citrus notes, while the sour cream gives the sauce a smooth texture. Since the chicken is already cooked, this recipe is ready in just 20 minutes.

**Recipe Details:**

- **Skill Level:** Easy
- **Prep Time:** 5 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 15 minutes
- **Total Time:** 20 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Main Ingredients:** Cooked chicken, fresh asparagus, olive oil, butter, chicken broth, sour cream, lemon juice, garlic powder, and optional Parmesan.
- **Recipe Source:** [Momcrieff – Lemon Garlic Chicken and Asparagus Skillet](https://momcrieff.com/lemon-garlic-chicken-and-asparagus-skillet/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

You can serve this skillet on its own for a lighter dinner or add cooked pasta to make it more filling. Broccoli and green beans are also good alternatives if you don't have asparagus available. For the smoothest sauce, keep the heat low after adding the sour cream and avoid bringing it to a boil.
