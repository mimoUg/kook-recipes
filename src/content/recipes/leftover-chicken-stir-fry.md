This leftover chicken stir fry is a quick way to turn cooked chicken into a colorful weeknight dinner. Broccoli, bell pepper and carrot are cooked until just tender, then tossed with a simple sauce of soy sauce, garlic, ginger and a touch of honey. Because the chicken is already cooked, it only needs to warm through at the end, so it stays juicy instead of turning dry.

**Recipe Details:**

- **Skill Level:** Easy
- **Prep Time:** 10 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 10 minutes
- **Total Time:** 20 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Main Ingredients:** Cooked chicken, broccoli, bell pepper, carrot, onion, garlic, ginger, soy sauce, honey, and sesame oil.
- **Recipe Source:** [The Spruce Eats – Chicken Stir Fry With Red and Green Peppers](https://www.thespruceeats.com/chicken-stir-fry-with-bell-peppers-694895), [Neil's Healthy Meals – Leftover Chicken Stir-Fry](https://neilshealthymeals.com/leftover-chicken-stir-fry/), [The Cooking Guy – Leftover Celery & Chicken Stir Fry](https://www.thecookingguy.com/recipes/leftover-celery-chicken-stir-fry)

Add the chicken last so it only heats through, and cut the vegetables to a similar size so they cook at the same speed. Snow peas, cabbage or frozen mixed vegetables work in place of the broccoli. Serve with rice, or stir in cooked noodles to make it a one-pan meal. Add chili flakes if you like heat.
