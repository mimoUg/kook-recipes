This classic eggplant parmesan skips the frying and bakes the eggplant instead, which is easier and uses less oil. The layers of roasted eggplant, tomato sauce and melted cheese taste just as comforting as the original.

**Recipe Details:**

- **Skill Level:** Medium
- **Total Time:** 1 hr 10 min
- **Servings:** 6
- **Main Ingredients:** Eggplant, tomato passata, mozzarella, parmesan, and olive oil.
- **Recipe Source:** [Bon Appetit - Classic Eggplant Parmesan](https://www.bonappetit.com/recipe/classic-eggplant-parmesan), [Serious Eats - Eggplant Parmesan](https://www.seriouseats.com/italian-style-eggplant-parmesan-melanzane-alla-parmigiana-recipe)

Roasting the eggplant first is what stops the dish going watery, so do not skip it. Resting for 10 minutes after baking helps it slice neatly. Leftovers keep for 3 days in the fridge and reheat well in the oven.
