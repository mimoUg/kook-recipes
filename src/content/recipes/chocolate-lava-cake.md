A chocolate lava cake is the sort of dessert that makes a meal feel like an occasion. Crack through the soft top and a river of warm chocolate spills out — and yet the whole thing takes less than half an hour.

## The secret to a molten centre

It comes down to timing. Pull the cakes out when the edges are set but the middle still has a gentle wobble. Every oven is different, so check them at 10 minutes the first time you make them.

## Make it ahead

You can prepare the filled ramekins up to a day in advance and keep them in the fridge. When you are ready for dessert, bake them straight from cold and add a couple of minutes to the timer.

## Serving ideas

Vanilla ice cream is the classic contrast, but crème fraîche, salted caramel sauce or a spoonful of raspberry coulis are all excellent. A few fresh raspberries cut through the richness beautifully.
