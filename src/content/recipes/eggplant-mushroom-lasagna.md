This eggplant and mushroom lasagna is the kind of vegetarian bake that nobody misses the meat in. Roasting the eggplant and browning the mushrooms first builds a deep, savoury flavour that carries the whole dish.

**Recipe Details:**

- **Skill Level:** Medium
- **Total Time:** 1 hr 20 min
- **Servings:** 8
- **Main Ingredients:** Eggplant, mushrooms, lasagna sheets, tomato passata, mozzarella, and parmesan.
- **Recipe Source:** [Martha Stewart - Mushroom and Eggplant Lasagna](https://www.marthastewart.com/1532367/mushroom-eggplant-lasagna), [Simple Italian Cooking - Vegetarian Eggplant Lasagna](https://www.simpleitaliancooking.com/easy-vegetarian-eggplant-lasagna-recipe-mushrooms/)

Brown the mushrooms in a hot pan without crowding so they caramelise instead of steaming. Resting before slicing keeps the layers intact. The lasagna freezes well, baked or unbaked, for up to 3 months.
