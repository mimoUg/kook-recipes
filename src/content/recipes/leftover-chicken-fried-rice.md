This leftover chicken fried rice is faster than takeout and ready in about 20 minutes. Cold rice is fried with garlic, eggs, peas and carrots, then tossed with cooked chicken and soy sauce. Day-old rice fries best because it is dry and stays separate.

**Recipe Details:**

- **Skill Level:** Easy
- **Prep Time:** 5 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 15 minutes
- **Total Time:** 20 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Main Ingredients:** Cooked chicken, cold rice, eggs, peas, carrots, soy sauce, garlic, and spring onions.
- **Recipe Source:** [PureWow – Leftover Chicken Fried Rice](https://www.purewow.com/recipes/leftover-chicken-fried-rice), [Platings and Pairings – Rotisserie Chicken Fried Rice](https://www.platingsandpairings.com/rotisserie-chicken-fried-rice/)

Cold, day-old rice is the key to good fried rice; fresh rice turns soft and clumpy. If you only have fresh rice, spread it on a tray and chill it for 30 minutes first. Keep the pan hot and do not crowd it, and add the chicken near the end so it warms through without drying out.
