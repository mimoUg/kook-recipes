---
title: Easy Leftover Chicken Curry (Ready in 25 Minutes)
description: >-
  Turn leftover chicken into a creamy, fragrant curry with pantry spices and
  coconut milk. Ready in 25 minutes, serve with rice or naan.
categories:
  - Dinner
totalTime: 30 min
servings: 4
difficulty: Easy
popular: false
published: false
publishedDate: 2026-11-27
ingredients:
  - 3 cups cooked chicken, shredded or cubed
  - 2 tbsp oil
  - 1 onion, finely diced
  - 3 cloves garlic, minced
  - 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated (or 1/2 tsp ground)
  - 2 tbsp curry powder
  - 1 can (14 oz / 400 ml) diced tomatoes
  - 1 can (13.5 oz / 400 ml) coconut milk
  - 1/2 cup chicken broth
  - 1 cup frozen peas or spinach
  - Salt, to taste
  - Juice of 1/2 lime
  - Fresh cilantro, to serve
steps:
  - title: Soften the onion
    description: Heat oil in a deep pan over medium heat. Cook onion 5 minutes until soft.
  - title: Add the aromatics
    description: Add garlic and ginger for 1 minute. Stir in curry powder for 30 seconds.
  - title: Make the sauce
    description: >-
      Add tomatoes, coconut milk and broth. Simmer 10 minutes until slightly
      thick.
  - title: Warm the chicken
    description: >-
      Add chicken and peas. Simmer 5 minutes, until the chicken is steaming hot
      throughout.
  - title: Season and serve
    description: >-
      Season with salt and lime juice. Top with cilantro. Serve over rice or
      with naan.
faq:
  - question: Can I use rotisserie chicken?
    answer: Yes, remove skin and bones first.
  - question: Can I freeze it?
    answer: >-
      Yes, cool it first and freeze in portions. Keeps 3 to 4 months in the
      freezer.
  - question: Will the chicken dry out?
    answer: No, the chicken only heats in the sauce, so add it near the end.
---
Cooked chicken is perfect for curry because it only needs to warm through in the sauce. This version builds flavor from onion, garlic, ginger and curry powder, then finishes with coconut milk. It works with roast, rotisserie or grilled chicken.

Tips: Add a spoon of yogurt off the heat for tang. Use chickpeas or potatoes to stretch it. Add chili flakes for heat.

Storage: Cool within 2 hours and store in a covered container. Keeps 3 to 4 days in the fridge or 3 to 4 months in the freezer (USDA FSIS). Reheat to 165 F and bring the sauce to a rolling boil.
