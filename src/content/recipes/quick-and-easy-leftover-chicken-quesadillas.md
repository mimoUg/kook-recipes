These quick and easy leftover chicken quesadillas from Layla Atik are perfect when you want something cheesy and satisfying without spending much time in the kitchen. The filling combines shredded cooked chicken with cheddar or Monterey Jack cheese, sour cream or cream cheese, garlic powder, and fresh cilantro. The creamy mixture is spread between flour tortillas and cooked until the outside becomes beautifully golden and crispy while the cheese melts inside.

**Recipe Details:**

- **Prep Time:** 5 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 10 minutes
- **Total Time:** 15 minutes
- **Servings:** 4 servings
- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **Original Source:** [Gimme Delicious – Leftover Chicken Quesadillas](https://gimmedelicious.com/quick-and-easy-leftover-chicken-quesadillas/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

This recipe is especially handy for busy weekdays, quick lunches, or after-school snacks. You can use sour cream for a lighter, tangier filling or cream cheese for extra richness. Feel free to add chopped green chilies, sautéed onions, or a little extra shredded cheese. Serve the finished quesadillas with salsa, guacamole, or your favorite dipping sauce.
