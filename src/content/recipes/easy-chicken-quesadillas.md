ingredients:
  - 4 large flour tortillas
  - 1 tablespoon olive oil plus extra as needed
  - 1 small bell pepper red or green
  - ⅓ cup finely diced onion
  - 1 ½ cups cooked shredded chicken or chopped chicken
  - 1 ounce taco seasoning 1 packet, or fajita seasoning
  - 2 cups shredded Monterey jack cheese or shredded Mexican cheese blend
steps:
  - title: '1'
    description: >
      Heat 1 tablespoon olive oil over medium-high heat. Cook onions 2-3 minutes
      or until softened. Add bell peppers and cook an additional 2-3 minutes.
  - title: '2'
    description: >
      Stir in chicken, seasoning and ¼ cup water. Simmer 3-4 minutes or until
      most of the liquid has evaporated.
  - title: '3'
    description: >
      Lay out the tortillas and sprinkle with ½ cup cheese on one half of the
      tortilla. Add ¼ of the chicken mixture. Fold half of the tortilla over the
      filling. Brush the outside of each tortilla with olive oil.
  - title: '4'
    description: >
      Heat a non-stick pan over medium-low heat (or a griddle). Lightly brown
      each quesadillas 3-4 minutes per side or until golden and cheese is
      melted.
  - title: '5'
    description: |
      Cool 2-3 minutes and cut each tortilla into 3 pieces.

The best part about this recipe is how easily you can customize the filling. Use leftover roasted chicken, rotisserie chicken, or shredded chicken from another meal. You can also add jalapeños for extra heat or swap Monterey Jack for cheddar. Serve the quesadillas with fresh salsa, guacamole, or sour cream for a delicious family lunch or an easy weeknight dinner.
