These blueberry lemon pancakes are what weekend mornings are for. The batter is light and fluffy, the blueberries burst as they cook, and the lemon zest keeps everything fresh rather than overly sweet.

## Tips for fluffy pancakes

- **Don't overmix.** Stir until the flour is just incorporated. Lumps are a good sign.
- **Rest the batter.** Five minutes on the counter gives the baking powder time to activate.
- **Use medium heat.** Too hot and the outside burns before the middle cooks; too cool and the pancakes turn pale and flat.
- **Don't press down.** Resist the urge to squash the pancakes with the spatula.

## Toppings we love

Maple syrup is the classic, but these pancakes are also lovely with Greek yoghurt, a spoonful of lemon curd, or a drizzle of honey and toasted almonds. Fresh raspberries work just as well as blueberries.

## Make it a brunch

Serve with crispy bacon, scrambled eggs and a pot of coffee. For a smaller batch, the recipe halves easily.
