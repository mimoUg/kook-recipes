This leftover chicken salad turns cooked chicken into a creamy, crunchy lunch in about 15 minutes. Celery, red onion and grapes add crunch and sweetness, and a simple mayonnaise and Greek yogurt dressing with lemon and Dijon holds it together. It works with roast, rotisserie or poached chicken.

**Recipe Details:**

- **Skill Level:** Easy
- **Prep Time:** 15 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 0 minutes
- **Total Time:** 15 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Main Ingredients:** Cooked chicken, celery, red onion, grapes, mayonnaise, Greek yogurt, lemon, and Dijon mustard.
- **Recipe Source:** [Allrecipes – Best Chicken Salad](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/8499/basic-chicken-salad/), [Don't Go Bacon My Heart – Leftover Chicken Salad](https://www.dontgobaconmyheart.co.uk/leftover-chicken-salad/)

Shred the chicken finely for sandwiches and dice it for a chunkier salad. Swap the grapes for chopped apple, dried cranberries or sweetcorn, and add toasted pecans for extra crunch. Chilling for half an hour lets the flavors blend.
