This Szechuan-style eggplant stir-fry is the dish that converts eggplant skeptics. Frying the pieces until golden makes them silky inside, and the garlic, chilli bean paste and vinegar sauce coats every piece.

**Recipe Details:**

- **Skill Level:** Easy
- **Total Time:** 30 min
- **Servings:** 4
- **Main Ingredients:** Eggplant, garlic, chilli bean paste, soy sauce, rice vinegar, and spring onions.
- **Recipe Source:** [Omnivore's Cookbook - Sichuan Eggplant Stir Fry](https://omnivorescookbook.com/sichuan-eggplant/), [Simply Recipes - Sichuan Eggplant](https://www.simplyrecipes.com/recipes/sichuan_eggplant/)

Salting the eggplant first draws out moisture so it fries instead of steaming. Chilli bean paste (doubanjiang) is in most large supermarkets or Asian grocers; for a milder dish, use half and add a splash of water.
