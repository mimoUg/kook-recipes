These Mini Chicken Tacos from Lily Payen are a fun and delicious way to transform leftover chicken into something the whole family can enjoy. Made with shredded cooked chicken, melted Colby Jack or Mexican cheese, petite diced tomatoes, garlic, and taco seasoning, the filling is packed with savory flavor. Everything is tucked into small corn tortillas and pan-fried until crispy and golden. Their bite-sized shape makes them especially appealing for little ones, but adults will love them too!
