ingredients:
  - 8 ounces penne pasta (about 2 1/2 cups dry)
  - 2 cups cooked chicken, chopped or shredded
  - 2 tablespoons butter
  - 2 cloves garlic, minced
  - 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  - 1 1/2 cups milk
  - 1/2 cup chicken broth
  - 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  - 1 teaspoon dried Italian herbs
  - 1 cup frozen peas or sweetcorn
  - 1 1/2 cups shredded cheese (mozzarella, cheddar or a mix), divided
  - Salt and black pepper

This leftover chicken pasta bake turns cooked chicken into a cheesy, bubbling family dinner. Penne is tossed with chicken in a creamy sauce made from butter, flour and milk, with garlic and a little tomato for flavor, then topped with cheese and baked until golden. It is a great way to use up roast or rotisserie chicken.

**Recipe Details:**

- **Skill Level:** Easy
- **Prep Time:** 15 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 30 minutes
- **Total Time:** 45 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Main Ingredients:** Cooked chicken, penne, butter, flour, milk, tomato paste, peas, and cheese.
- **Recipe Source:** [Mama Loves to Cook – Leftover Chicken Pasta Bake](https://mamalovestocook.com/leftover-chicken-pasta-bake/), [Netmums – Leftover Chicken Pasta Bake](https://www.netmums.com/recipes/leftover-chicken-pasta-bake)

Undercook the pasta slightly because it keeps softening in the oven. Sweetcorn, broccoli or spinach can replace the peas. For extra crunch, sprinkle breadcrumbs over the cheese. You can assemble the dish ahead and bake it when you are ready, adding a few minutes if it goes in cold.
