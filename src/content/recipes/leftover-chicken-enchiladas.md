These leftover chicken enchiladas turn cooked chicken into a comforting baked dinner in about 40 minutes. Shredded chicken is mixed with cheese and a little enchilada sauce, rolled in tortillas, covered with more sauce and cheese, and baked until bubbling. It works with roast, rotisserie or poached chicken.

**Recipe Details:**

- **Skill Level:** Easy
- **Prep Time:** 15 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 25 minutes
- **Total Time:** 40 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Main Ingredients:** Cooked chicken, tortillas, enchilada sauce, cheese, cumin, and onion.
- **Recipe Source:** [NYT Cooking – Chicken Enchiladas](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1024437-chicken-enchiladas), [Simply Recipes – Easy Chicken Skillet Enchiladas](https://www.simplyrecipes.com/recipes/easy_chicken_skillet_enchiladas/)

Warm the tortillas in a dry pan or in the microwave under a damp paper towel so they roll without splitting. Corn tortillas give the classic flavor; flour tortillas are softer and easier to roll. Add a can of drained black beans or some sweetcorn to the filling to stretch it further.
