This spicy Thai basil fried rice is the reason to keep leftover rice in the fridge. It is fast, deeply savoury and packed with the kind of chilli-garlic fragrance that makes people wander into the kitchen before it is even plated.

## The secret is cold rice

Freshly cooked rice steams instead of frying, which makes the dish gluey. Spread cooked rice on a tray, cool it completely, then refrigerate it until you are ready to cook. Day-old rice is ideal.

## Make it your own

Add cooked chicken, prawns, tofu or leftover vegetables. A handful of frozen peas or sweetcorn can go in with the rice. Keep the sauces balanced: salty from soy and fish sauce, a little sweet from sugar, and bright from lime at the table.

## Serving ideas

Serve it as a standalone weeknight dinner, or alongside grilled chicken skewers, a crisp cucumber salad or a fried egg on top. A cold lager or lime soda is a very good match.
