title: Easy Leftover Chicken Tacos (15 Minutes)
description: >-
  Crispy, saucy leftover chicken tacos ready in 15 minutes. Use roast,
  rotisserie or grilled chicken and your favorite toppings.
categories:
  - Dinner
totalTime: 15 min
servings: 4
difficulty: Easy
popular: false
published: false
publishedDate: 2026-11-27
ingredients:
  - 3 cups shredded cooked chicken
  - 1 tbsp oil
  - >-
    1 tbsp taco seasoning (or 1 tsp chili powder, 1/2 tsp cumin, 1/2 tsp garlic
    powder, 1/2 tsp paprika)
  - 1/2 cup chicken broth or salsa
  - 8 small tortillas
  - >-
    Toppings: shredded cabbage or lettuce, diced tomato, avocado, cheese, lime,
    sour cream or yogurt, cilantro
steps:
  - title: Season the chicken
    description: >-
      Heat oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add chicken and seasoning, stir 1
      minute.
  - title: Simmer
    description: >-
      Add broth or salsa. Simmer 3 to 5 minutes until the chicken is hot
      throughout and the liquid mostly absorbed.
  - title: Warm the tortillas
    description: Warm tortillas in a dry pan 20 to 30 seconds per side.
  - title: Fill and serve
    description: Fill with chicken and toppings. Serve with lime.
faq:
  - question: Can I use cold chicken straight from the fridge?
    answer: Yes, but heat it until steaming hot.
  - question: Can I make the filling ahead?
    answer: >-
      Yes. Store it covered in the fridge for up to 3 to 4 days and reheat until
      hot throughout.
---
Tacos are the fastest way to use cooked chicken. Warm the chicken in a quick seasoning sauce so it stays juicy, then pile on toppings.

Tips: Crisp the chicken in the pan for a few minutes for browned edges. Make a double batch for quesadillas. Use corn tortillas for gluten-free, and check the label.

Storage: Store the chicken filling and toppings separately, covered, 3 to 4 days in the fridge (USDA FSIS). Reheat the filling to 165 F.
