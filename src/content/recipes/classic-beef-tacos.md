Taco night should be easy, messy and full of colour. This classic beef filling is smoky and savoury, with just enough heat to keep things interesting. Pile it into warm tortillas with crunchy cabbage, creamy avocado and plenty of lime.

## The key to good taco meat

Brown the beef properly before adding the spices. Those browned bits are where the flavour lives. Tomato paste adds depth, and a splash of stock keeps the meat juicy rather than dry.

## Set up a taco bar

Let everyone build their own. Put the beef, tortillas and toppings in separate bowls and let the table do the rest. Good options include pickled red onion, grated cheese, salsa verde, shredded lettuce and extra lime.

## Leftovers

Any leftover beef makes a great filling for quesadillas, nachos or a quick rice bowl. Store it in the fridge for up to 3 days.
