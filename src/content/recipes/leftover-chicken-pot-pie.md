ingredients:
  - 2 refrigerated pie crusts (for a 9-inch pie)
  - 3 tablespoons butter
  - 1 small onion, diced
  - 2 carrots, diced
  - 2 celery stalks, diced
  - 1/3 cup all-purpose flour
  - 1 1/2 cups chicken broth
  - 3/4 cup milk
  - 3 cups cooked chicken, chopped
  - 1 cup frozen peas
  - 1 teaspoon dried thyme
  - Salt and black pepper
  - 1 egg, beaten, for brushing
steps:
  - title: '1'
    description: |
      Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Press one pie crust into a 9-inch pie dish.
  - title: '2'
    description: |
      Melt the butter in a large pan. Cook the onion, carrots and celery for 6 to 8 minutes, until soft.
    image: >-
      https://res.cloudinary.com/xuvhoikp/image/upload/q_auto,w_1600/14-46-pie-step-2-v2-d00e11f2.png
  - title: '3'
    description: |
      Stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute.
  - title: '4'
    description: |
      Slowly whisk in the broth and milk. Simmer for 3 to 4 minutes, until thick.
  - title: '5'
    description: |
      Stir in the chicken, peas and thyme. Season well with salt and pepper and let the filling cool for 10 minutes.
    image: >-

  - title: '6'
    description: |
      Pour the filling into the crust, cover with the second crust, trim and crimp the edges, and cut a few slits in the top.
  - title: '7'
    description: |
      Brush with the beaten egg and bake for 30 to 35 minutes, until deep golden. Rest for 10 minutes before slicing.

This leftover chicken pot pie fills a flaky crust with cooked chicken, peas, carrots and a creamy sauce made from butter, flour and broth. Store-bought pie crust keeps it simple. It bakes in about 35 minutes and uses up roast or rotisserie chicken in a cozy family dinner.

**Recipe Details:**

- **Skill Level:** Easy
- **Prep Time:** 25 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 35 minutes
- **Total Time:** 60 minutes
- **Servings:** 6
- **Main Ingredients:** Cooked chicken, pie crust, carrots, celery, peas, butter, flour, chicken broth, and milk.
- **Recipe Source:** [Food52 – Leftover Chicken Pot Pie](https://food52.com/recipes/64421-leftover-chicken-pot-pie), [Allrecipes – Chicken Pot Pie Made with Leftovers](https://www.allrecipes.com/chicken-pot-pie-made-with-leftovers-7972090)

Let the filling cool a little before it goes into the crust so the bottom stays crisp. Swap in sweetcorn, potato or mushrooms, and use puff pastry on top if you prefer a lighter lid.
