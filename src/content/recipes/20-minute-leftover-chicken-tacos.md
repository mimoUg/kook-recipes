These leftover chicken tacos are on the table in 20 minutes and work with any cooked chicken, from roast to rotisserie. The chicken is warmed with taco seasoning so it tastes freshly cooked, then piled into soft tortillas with the classic toppings.

**Recipe Details:**

- **Skill Level:** Easy
- **Total Time:** 20 min
- **Servings:** 4
- **Main Ingredients:** Cooked chicken, taco seasoning, flour tortillas, lettuce, tomato, and cheese.
- **Recipe Source:** [BBC Food - Leftover Chicken Tacos](https://www.bbc.co.uk/food/recipes/leftover_chicken_tacos_99324), [Effortless Foodie - Leftover Chicken Tacos](https://www.effortlessfoodie.com/leftover-chicken-tacos/)

A splash of water in the pan keeps the chicken from drying out while it warms. Set the toppings out and let everyone build their own. Leftover seasoned chicken keeps in the fridge for 2 days and reheats well in a dry pan.
