ingredients:
  - 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  - 4 cloves garlic, minced
  - 1 tbsp all-purpose flour
  - 1 cup low-sodium chicken broth
  - 1/2 cup heavy cream
  - 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  - 1 tsp apple cider vinegar
  - 3 cups shredded leftover rotisserie chicken, skin removed
  - 1/4 tsp black pepper, freshly ground
  - 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped, for garnish
  - Salt, to taste
steps:
  - title: '1'
    description: >
      Gather and prepare all your ingredients first (mise en place). Shred the
      chicken, mince the garlic, and chop the parsley. This recipe moves
      quickly, so you’ll want everything ready.
  - title: '2'
    description: >
      In a large skillet or pan, melt the butter over medium heat. Add the
      minced garlic and sauté for about 30-60 seconds until fragrant. Be careful
      not to let the garlic burn, or it will become bitter.
  - title: '3'
    description: >
      Sprinkle the flour over the garlic and butter, and whisk constantly for
      one minute to cook out the raw flour taste. This forms a “roux” which will
      thicken our sauce.
  - title: '4'
    description: >
      Slowly pour in the chicken broth while whisking continuously to prevent
      lumps. Bring the mixture to a simmer, then add the heavy cream, Parmesan
      cheese, and the key ingredient: apple cider vinegar.
  - title: '5'
    description: >
      Continue to simmer gently, stirring occasionally, for 3-5 minutes, until
      the sauce has thickened enough to coat the back of a spoon. Season with
      black pepper.
  - title: '6'
    description: >
      Add the shredded rotisserie chicken to the skillet. Stir gently to coat
      all the chicken in the creamy sauce. Let it heat through for another 2-3
      minutes.
  - title: '7'
    description: >
      Taste the sauce and add salt if needed. Remember, your chicken is already
      salty. Remove from heat, garnish with fresh parsley, and serve
      immediately.

This creamy garlic chicken skillet is a simple way to transform leftover rotisserie chicken into a satisfying dinner. Shredded chicken is coated in a smooth sauce made with butter, fresh garlic, chicken broth, heavy cream, and Parmesan cheese. A little apple cider vinegar adds brightness and balances the richness of the sauce. Since the chicken is already cooked, you only need to prepare the sauce and warm everything together in one skillet.
