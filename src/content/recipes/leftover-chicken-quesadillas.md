These leftover chicken quesadillas are a 15-minute lunch or easy dinner. Cooked chicken is mixed with cheese and a little seasoning, folded into tortillas and toasted in a dry pan until crisp and golden. It works with roast, rotisserie or poached chicken.

**Recipe Details:**

- **Skill Level:** Easy
- **Prep Time:** 5 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 10 minutes
- **Total Time:** 15 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Main Ingredients:** Cooked chicken, flour tortillas, cheese, chili powder, cumin, and spring onion.
- **Recipe Source:** [Gimme Delicious – Quick and Easy Leftover Chicken Quesadillas](https://gimmedelicious.com/quick-and-easy-leftover-chicken-quesadillas/), [NYT Cooking – Chicken Quesadillas](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1025033-chicken-quesadillas)

A thin layer of cheese on both sides of the filling is what holds a quesadilla together. Keep the heat at medium so the tortilla crisps before the cheese runs out, and do not overfill. Leftover quesadillas reheat well in a dry pan for a minute per side.
