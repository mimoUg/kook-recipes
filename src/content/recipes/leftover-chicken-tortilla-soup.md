This leftover chicken tortilla soup is a warm, smoky bowl that comes together in about 30 minutes. Onion, garlic and spices are cooked in a pot, then simmered with chicken broth, tomatoes, black beans and corn. The cooked chicken goes in near the end so it stays tender, and crispy tortilla strips, avocado, cheese and lime on top turn it into a full meal.

**Recipe Details:**

- **Skill Level:** Easy
- **Prep Time:** 10 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 20 minutes
- **Total Time:** 30 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Main Ingredients:** Cooked chicken, chicken broth, tomatoes, black beans, corn, cumin, chili powder, lime, and corn tortillas.
- **Recipe Source:** [Simply Recipes – Chicken Tortilla Soup](https://www.simplyrecipes.com/recipes/tortilla_soup/), [Cooks with Soul – Rotisserie Chicken Tortilla Soup](https://cookswithsoul.com/rotisserie-chicken-tortilla-soup/), [A Couple Cooks – Chicken Tortilla Soup](https://www.acouplecooks.com/chicken-tortilla-soup/)

Baking the tortilla strips keeps them crisp for the whole bowl, so add them just before serving. Leftover turkey works instead of chicken, and a spoonful of chipotle in adobo adds smoky heat. If you reheat leftover soup, bring it to a rolling boil, as the USDA advises for soups and sauces ([FSIS](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/leftovers-and-food-safety)).
