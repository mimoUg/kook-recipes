This creamy leftover chicken skillet is a one-pan dinner in 20 minutes. The sauce is a simple mix of garlic, broth and cream, and the cooked chicken only needs a few minutes to warm through, so it stays juicy instead of drying out.

**Recipe Details:**

- **Skill Level:** Easy
- **Total Time:** 20 min
- **Servings:** 4
- **Main Ingredients:** Cooked chicken, butter, garlic, chicken broth, double cream, and Italian herbs.
- **Recipe Source:** [Salt & Lavender - Creamy Garlic Chicken](https://www.saltandlavender.com/creamy-garlic-chicken/), [Cafe Delites - Creamy Garlic Chicken](https://cafedelites.com/creamy-garlic-chicken-breasts/)

Keep the heat at a gentle simmer once the cream goes in so the sauce stays smooth. If it gets too thick, loosen it with a splash of broth. Serve over rice, pasta or mashed potatoes to catch every bit of sauce.
