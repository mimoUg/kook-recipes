This creamy Tuscan garlic chicken is the kind of dinner that looks impressive but comes together in one pan on a weeknight. Golden chicken breasts are nestled into a garlicky cream sauce with sun-dried tomatoes, Parmesan and a generous handful of spinach.

## Why you'll love this recipe

- **One pan, minimal washing up.** The chicken sears first, then the sauce is built in the same skillet.
- **Big flavour from simple ingredients.** Sun-dried tomatoes and Parmesan do most of the heavy lifting.
- **Ready in about 35 minutes.** It is quick enough for a Tuesday and special enough for guests.
- **Flexible.** Swap the spinach for kale, add mushrooms, or serve it with pasta instead of potatoes.

## What to serve with it

Crusty bread is perfect for mopping up the sauce. For something more substantial, serve the chicken over linguine, creamy mashed potatoes or a simple green salad with lemon dressing.

## A note on the sauce

Let the cream come to a gentle simmer before adding the Parmesan so the sauce thickens evenly. If it gets too thick, loosen it with a splash of chicken stock. If you want a little extra brightness, finish with a squeeze of lemon and plenty of black pepper.
