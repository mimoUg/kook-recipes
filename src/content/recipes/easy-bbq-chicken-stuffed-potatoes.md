These BBQ chicken stuffed potatoes turn cooked chicken and a few store-cupboard staples into a proper dinner. Bake the potatoes ahead, or use the microwave to cook them in about 10 minutes, and dinner is done in 15.

**Recipe Details:**

- **Skill Level:** Easy
- **Total Time:** 15 min (with pre-baked potatoes)
- **Servings:** 4
- **Main Ingredients:** Baked potatoes, cooked chicken, BBQ sauce, and cheddar cheese.
- **Recipe Source:** [Food Network - BBQ Chicken Baked Potatoes](https://www.foodnetwork.com/recipes/ree-drummond/bbq-chicken-baked-potatoes-5541483), [Idaho Potato - Barbecue Chicken Stuffed Baked Potato](https://idahopotato.com/recipes/barbecue-chicken-stuffed-baked-potato)

Warm the chicken with the sauce before stuffing so the filling is hot all the way through. Any cooked chicken works, and leftover pulled pork is a good swap. Add coleslaw on top if you want some crunch.
